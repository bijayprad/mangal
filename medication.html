  • Ecosprin (aspirin) + Stiloz (cilostazol): taking medicines that affect platelets together may increase bleeding risk. The combination may be intentional; confirm it with the prescriber and do not stop either medicine on your own.
  • Blood pressure medicines: the current list includes medicines that lower blood pressure. Because blood pressure was still 160/100 mmHg at discharge and the patient has a pacemaker, monitoring and clinician review are important.
  • Clonidine (Lonid): do not abruptly stop it without medical advice.
  • Insulin: verify the exact Humalog Mix product, units, and meal timing on the label. Monitor glucose as directed by the treating team.
  • Diuretics (Rexter/torsemide in the current list; furosemide in the discharge report): these are different medicines. Do not assume they are interchangeable or take both unless specifically prescribed.
