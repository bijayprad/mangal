1. Brief medical summary

The supplied records document a diagnosis of chronic myeloproliferative disorder, considered most consistent with essential thrombocythemia (ET), on 7 February 2012. The work-up recorded markedly elevated platelet counts, a detected JAK2 V617F mutation, a bone-marrow pattern suggesting ET, and no BCR-ABL transcript detected in the test performed.

Treatment records list hydroxyurea (Hydrea; dose adjusted according to CBC results) and low-dose aspirin (Ecosprin 75 mg daily), with a short course of Trentel also noted in the initial advice. CBC results from 2012–2015 show substantial variation in platelet, white-cell and hemoglobin values during follow-up.

Medical Record: Mother died on 18 September 2023.

3. Key diagnostic investigations (February 2012)

Bone-marrow examination

  • Biopsy described mildly hypercellular marrow (approximately 50–60%) with trilineage hematopoiesis and myeloid/megakaryocytic predominance.
  • Megakaryocytic hyperplasia with many hyperlobulated megakaryocytes was reported.
  • Reticulin stain showed no increased fibrosis (MF-0 in the report).
  • Overall opinion: myeloproliferative neoplasm, probably essential thrombocythemia.

Molecular and chromosome tests

  • JAK2 V617F: detected.
  • BCR-ABL translocation assay: not detected in the tested specimen.
  • Karyotype: 46,XX in 20 metaphases; reported as a normal female karyotype.
